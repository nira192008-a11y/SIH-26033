## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Direct Selling: Farmers can sell their products directly to customers through a mobile app or website.



Fair Pricing: Farmers can decide reasonable prices and earn better profits.



Eliminate Middlemen: Reduce unnecessary intermediaries between farmers and consumers.



Online Marketplace: Customers can view available products, compare prices, and place orders online.



Direct Delivery: Arrange delivery from farms to consumers using local transport.



Price Transparency: Display farmers' selling prices so consumers know where their money goes.

## Technical Approach
Technology Stack: HTML + CSS + JavaScript + Python Flask + SQLite/MySQL.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Technical Feasibility: Can be developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python Flask, and MySQL.

2. Economic Feasibility: Low development cost using open-source technologies and reduced middleman commissions.

3. Operational Feasibility: Easy registration, product uploading, ordering, and delivery management.

4. Market Viability: Growing demand for fresh, affordable agricultural products.

5. Scalability: Can expand to more farmers, customers, and locations.

## Impact and Benefits
Higher Farmer Income: Farmers earn more by selling directly to consumers.



Lower Consumer Prices: Reduces additional costs charged by intermediaries.



Fresh Products: Provides fresh and quality agricultural products.



Transparency: Ensures fair pricing and direct communication.



Reduced Food Waste: Improves product distribution and reduces delays.



Digital Empowerment: Helps farmers use technology to grow their businesses.

## Research and References
Research Findings:
- Government's e-NAM platform supports online agricultural trading.

- Digital marketplaces improve farmers' access to buyers.

- Transparent pricing helps farmers make better selling decisions.

- Direct marketing can reduce unnecessary intermediaries.
